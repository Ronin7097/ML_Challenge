DARPA final model: dense owner retrieval plus incoming-context LightGBM

Encoder: IBM Granite embedding 97M multilingual r2, revision
835ad14087e140460703cf0fae09f97d469d65c2, Apache-2.0. Fine-tuned
on the supplied challenge training pairs. Saved model SHA-256:
192974ed0c8ed02ee2c48dad6dbf3d7447143b18fe7cd91d2bb8b5a106b931bd

Matching: one 76-feature LightGBM pair model and five 90-feature LightGBM
incoming-context models, fitted on separate challenge training roles.
The frozen decision threshold is 0.62. Exact model hashes and the
pre-reserve selection record are in reports/frozen_policy.json.

No Qwen weights are used by the selected final inference pipeline. The
Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B adapter was an ablation that scored worse on the
held-out tuning cohort and was rejected.

The model is below the challenge's 8-billion-parameter limit. The Granite
base and derivative weights are Apache-2.0; DARPA-authored code is MIT.
